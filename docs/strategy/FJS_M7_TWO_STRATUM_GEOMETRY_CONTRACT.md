## Stratum A: balanced real control

Stratum A is the exact M6 seasoned-universe method and point-in-time cap-ranked
top 60. It must pass every unchanged computed geometry gate other than
`natural_missing_cells_min`. That one requirement is not weakened or deleted:
it applies to Stratum B, whose explicit role is to validate naturally
incomplete geometry. The control must remain the complete M6 panel.

## Stratum B: natural-missingness stress

Start from the same M6 seasoned candidate pool at the same 2013-01 formation
date. Retain only candidates with a valid lagged-cap observation no more than
10 calendar days old and no more than `floor(0.10 * N)` missing dates, where
`N` is the factor-calendar date count. Rank candidates deterministically by:

1. any natural missingness before complete histories;
2. missing-date count descending;
3. lagged market capitalization descending; and
4. PERMNO ascending.

Traverse that frozen order once. Accept a candidate only if doing so keeps no
more than `60 - 57 = 3` selected names missing on any date and keeps total
missing cells no greater than `floor(0.10 * N * 60)`. Stop at 60 names. The
selection must contain at least one natural missing cell or fail closed.

The shared M6 eligibility already guarantees the same most-recent 78 complete
five-date anchor weeks and at least 500 pairwise observations by
inclusion-exclusion. The new per-date and total capacities guarantee the
unchanged 57-assets-per-date and 10% panel-missing gates. These rules are
frozen before the M7 real result and use only information available at the
formation date.
